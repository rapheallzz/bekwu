OUR PERSPECTIVE

Closing the Gap.

The way audiences discover, consume, and engage with content now moves fluidly across platforms, markets, and borders. Yet the systems used to measure and value these audiences have not kept pace.

This creates a gap. A gap between what audiences are actually doing—and how they are being understood, positioned, and monetized. Our work is centered on closing that gap.

SPECIALIZED LENS

Multicultural & Global Diaspora Lens

Specialized expertise in Black, African, diaspora and multicultural consumers - with the ability to apply that cultural intelligence to audience, product, experience and growth questions.

We analyze how Black audiences across the U.S. and international markets, including African, Caribbean, and cross-border communities, shape product consumption, influence cultural trends, and drive economic value.

These consumers and users are often:

  • Under Measured
  • Misclassified
  • Or Undervalued in Digital Products & Media Systems

We focus on identifying where those gaps exist and what they mean for product, positioning, and long-term growth. These audiences are not niche. They are central to how culture, digital experiences, and global markets evolve.
