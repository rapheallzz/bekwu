About

Barbara Shaibu is the founder of Békwu Consults, an audience intelligence and revenue strategy firm operating at the intersection of culture, data, and media.

Her work spans audience measurement, network strategy, and revenue enablement across linear, streaming, and digital ecosystems, with experience across leading media organizations including Nielsen and BET.

Békwu Consults works broadly across media companies, platforms, brands, and agencies, with a distinct lens on how Black and global audiences shape consumption, influence markets, and drive economic value.
